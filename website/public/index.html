A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Open source.
Open answers.

Ask us on GitHub
Can I keep using Docker and Compose?+

Yes. Skiff provides a Docker-compatible API and a skiff Docker context. Keep your existing Dockerfiles, CLI, and Compose workflows. Compatibility is an ongoing project; see the feature list and known limitations for the exact supported behavior.

Do I need a Cloudflare account?+

Only for sharing on your own domain. Temporary Quick Tunnel links don’t require an account or domain. Both sharing modes require cloudflared on your computer. Custom domains must be managed in your Cloudflare account. Local containers work without Cloudflare.

What happens when I stop sharing?+

Skiff stops its local tunnel process, so that share stops serving your app. Your Cloudflare tunnel and DNS record remain in your account for reuse. You can manage or delete those resources in Cloudflare. Treat a public link as public access to the service you choose to expose.

Is Skiff ready for production?+

Skiff is in early development. Use the preview builds to try local workflows and share feedback. Published macOS releases will use Developer ID signing and notarization; Windows installers are currently unsigned. Review the known limitations before depending on Skiff for important workloads.

Why is Skiff an independent project?+

Skiff began after Kiri’s maintainer declined our optional Cloudflare Tunnel integration, which gave end users control over the hosting and base domains of shared endpoints. So we took the fork in the road. We’re continuing that work independently, with user-controlled sharing at the center. A public link shouldn’t come with a company town attached. Skiff retains upstream’s Apache-2.0 attribution. Read the proposal, maintainer response, and attribution.
